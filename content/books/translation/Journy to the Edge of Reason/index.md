## About the book

Stephen Budiansky tells the story of Kurt Gödel, the brilliant and troubled logician whose incompleteness theorems transformed modern mathematics. Drawing on Gödel's letters and writings, the biography follows him through the intellectual world of Vienna and into the Institute for Advanced Study at Princeton, where he formed a close friendship with Albert Einstein.

Alongside the life, the book explains why Gödel's proof—that a sufficiently powerful formal system contains truths it cannot prove—continues to matter in mathematics, philosophy, and computer science. This Persian translation brings both the human portrait and the intellectual history together.
