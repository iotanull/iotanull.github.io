## About the book

Peter J. Feibelman addresses the professional lessons that scientific training often leaves implicit. He explains how to choose advisers and positions, define an independent research direction, communicate results clearly, prepare strong applications and proposals, and make deliberate decisions across academia, government laboratories, and industry.

This Persian translation presents the book as a compact field guide for graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and anyone approaching the uncertain first stages of a research career.
