## About the book

David Bohm examines how causal laws and chance descriptions work together in modern physics. Moving from the mechanistic picture of classical physics to quantum theory, he questions the idea that indeterminism must be the final form of physical explanation and develops a broader, layered view of natural law.

The book is both an argument for Bohm's causal approach to quantum mechanics and a wider inquiry into what physical theories mean when they describe events as necessary, probable, or random. This Persian translation makes that classic debate available to readers interested in physics and the philosophy of science.
