## About the book

Tobias Hürter recounts the birth of modern physics through the intertwined lives of Marie Curie, Max Planck, Albert Einstein, Niels Bohr, Werner Heisenberg, Erwin Schrödinger, Wolfgang Pauli, and their contemporaries. The story presents quantum theory not as the achievement of one isolated genius, but as the result of collaboration, rivalry, disagreement, and repeated acts of imagination.

Set against two world wars and the emergence of the atomic age, the book connects scientific ideas to the people and political pressures that shaped them. IO's Persian translation is based on David Shaw's English translation of Hürter's German work.
